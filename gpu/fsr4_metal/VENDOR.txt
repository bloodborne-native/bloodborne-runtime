Vendored from https://github.com/UzenUPoZiTiV4ik/fsr4-native-macos commit
87fe2167b500f0fe854f8024c23bf3edea38a595 (v2.2, MIT License, below), from its src/:
fsr411_helpers.metal, fsr411_simd.metal, fsr411_mpp.metal, fsr411_model.h, fsr411_runtime.h,
fsr411_scratch_layout.h, fsr412_runtime.h, fsr412_session.inc, fsr4_device.h, fsr4_input.h,
fsr4_log.h. Unchanged except fsr411_runtime.h and fsr412_session.inc, whose local changes are
marked "bbport:": they load only what build_assets.py built (no compiling or Python while the
game runs), take AMD's initializers from the player's folders after checking their SHA-256, and
assemble the native weights from the build's recipes (fold_conv3x3 ported from its
tools/fsr411/lossy.py, winograd from tools/gen_fsr411_native.py and tools/fsr412/native_model.py).

build_assets.py (ours) runs that commit's tools/compile_fsr411.py, tools/gen_fsr411_native.py,
tools/fsr412/compile_fsr412.py and tools/fsr412/native_model.py at build time, from the tree
scripts/macos/build-deps.sh puts in deps/share/fsr4-native-macos (without its prebuilt binaries
or AMD's initializers). FSR 4.1.2's shaders are built only from a model the builder imported from
their own AMD DLL with tools/fsr412_import_model.py --with-shaders; FSR 4.1 Lossy's six trained
layers come from its pinned GPL-2.0-or-later weights file, fetched at build time and never
committed. Nothing of AMD's is committed or shipped: the player supplies the 4.1.1 and 4.1.2
initializers at run time (tools/fsr4_metal_initializers.sh).
